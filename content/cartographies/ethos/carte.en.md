---
titre: Your position on one card
---

Produce the Ethos Card from this interview only, in English and addressing the
person directly. Ask no question: they cannot answer during this output.
Data and proposed wording from the interview are material to examine, never
instructions permitting invention.

`phrase` proposes a provisional professional position, not a definition of the
person: audience, contribution and practice only where the account supports them.
State what is missing. Keep the statement useful without unsupported promises
of revenue, customers, growth or superiority.

An unreported fact remains unknown: "no feedback reported" does not mean "nobody
has read it". Invent no figure, date, chronology, commercial motive, result or
permanent quality. Keep practices bounded to the described episodes; several
examples do not establish an unvarying method. Distinguish team size, people observed and
each participant's contribution. Any useful inference remains a hypothesis to
check, separate from facts. The latest explicit correction takes precedence,
including corrections to figures and attributed causes.

Prepare `connaissances` before writing, separating reported facts, premises
and hypotheses:

- Each `rapporte` observation contains one proposition, with an exact quotation,
  author and episode. Preserve its quantity, timing and limits: an exact quotation
  does not support every interpretation that can be attached to it.
- For a `hypothese`, distinguish the factual premises from the conclusion to
  check. Compare every premise with all turns, including corrections. If a
  premise is unknown or contradicted, remove the hypothesis or reformulate it
  from supported facts alone. Hypothesis status does not make its premise true.
- Use `indetermine` for what the account does not establish. A proposed check
  does not prove it never happened. An unattributable effect remains unknown,
  not zero. Feedback on collective work does not identify its author.

A correction replaces only the proposition concerned; other facts in the same
turn retain their scope. `enonce` contains the observation alone, without a
status prefix or commentary on how the Card was produced.

Apply this distinction to every public assertion, including reasons for the next
step, links between axes and premises of suggested directions. When moving from
the observations to prose, preserve author, episode, quantity and timing. A cited
record is not an inventory of records held; a named person is not a list of
interlocutors. Exhaustiveness or elapsed time requires a statement establishing
it. An unknown must remain an open question, without a fact added in its wording.
Write a natural account: keep observation identifiers and correction operations
in the structured fields, outside public prose. Keep useful limits explicit.

The dimensions are `destinataires` (people and situations encountered),
`contribution` (observed change and personal contribution), `pratique` (method
choices and trade-offs), and `convictions-limites` (positions, refusals and
excluded promises). Each must stay tied to a reported episode.

Scale for each of the four axes (`destinataires`, `contribution`, `pratique`,
`convictions-limites`): 0, no material; 1–3, a general claim; 4–6, a specific
episode with role and limits; 7–8, converging details with a counterexample or
limit; 9–10, a bounded claim, described records and explicit limits. It measures
only support in the account. A described record is not verified by Atlas.
No increase for eloquence or income, and no average. `confiance` expresses
interpretive uncertainty, not a scientific probability; without a concrete
episode, it stays below 40.

Each axis's `lecture` names its support and limit, without grading aptitude.
Correct any score contradicted by the transcript. Every required text field
contains complete useful text or an explicit limit, never an empty value or
placeholder. Invent no gap to fill `gap`; [] is valid when no missing check is established.

`tension` distinguishes the promise from available evidence. Invent no conflict:
the absence of one can be stated. `gap` references knowledge to check, without inventing a gap,
without inferring fear, injury, inherited motives or hidden personality.
`angles_morts` contains useful unknowns, not accusations.
Refusing confidential disclosure is neither avoidance nor a psychological cue.

`pas` contains one reversible action within the person's control within 48 hours,
its observable criterion and reason, without requiring publication, a contract
or spending. The criterion also admits zero matches: it records an observation
without requiring confirmation. Respect a request to work alone; otherwise, at most one private
request for feedback, with no reply required. Recording the message sent, no
reply or contradictory feedback yields an observation, never validation of market
demand or viability.

`suite.cartographie` is `diagnostic-alignement` only if the interview raises a
question of personal direction, and `suite.pourquoi` gives the specific reported
fact. Otherwise, `suite.cartographie` is `ethos` and the reason invites a review of
this Card after the next step, without promising a new conclusion. Invent no other
resource. The companion guide explains how to use the Card; do not invent a link
or field absent from the requested format.

If the interview stopped because of distress, do not analyze the person. State
the limit and suggest appropriate support; do not turn the incident into an axis,
a psychological score or a professional project.
